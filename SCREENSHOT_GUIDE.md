# 📸 Screenshot Guide for Your Portfolio

To make your `README.md` look professional, you need to take 5 specific screenshots and save them in a folder named `screenshots` inside your project root.

## 1. Create the Folder
Create a new folder named `screenshots` in your main project directory:
`C:\Users\hp\ALL PROJECTS\shopApp\screenshots\`

## 2. Take These 5 Screenshots

### Image 1: `dashboard_preview.png`
*   **Where to go**: The main **Dashboard** or **Reports Overview** screen.
*   **What to show**: The summary cards (Revenue, Profit) and the main sales chart. This is the "Hero" image that people see first.

### Image 2: `inventory.png`
*   **Where to go**: **Inventory Screen** -> **Analytics** or **Movements** tab.
*   **What to show**: The table showing stock movements or the charts showing inventory value. This proves you handle complex data.

### Image 3: `finance.png`
*   **Where to go**: **Reports Screen** -> **Financials** or **Aging Report** tab.
*   **What to show**: The **Aging Report** table (showing overdue invoices) or the **Cash Flow** chart. This highlights the "ERP" nature of your app.

### Image 4: `customer.png`
*   **Where to go**: **Customers Screen** -> Click "View" on a customer with a balance.
*   **What to show**: The **Customer Details Modal** showing the "Credit Limit", "Current Balance" (preferably in red/green), and the "Payment History" tab.

### Image 5: `sales.png`
*   **Where to go**: **Sales Screen** (The POS interface).
*   **What to show**: The cart populated with items, and the "Payment Method" dropdown open showing the **"Credit (Pay Later)"** option.

## 3. Save & Commit
1.  Save the images with the exact names above (`inventory.png`, `finance.png`, etc.) into the `screenshots` folder.
2.  Push the `screenshots` folder and the `README.md` to GitHub.
